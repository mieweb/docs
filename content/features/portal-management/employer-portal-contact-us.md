Employers may optionally configure feedback forms within [Pollenate](https://pollenate.dev/login) to display within the Contact Us side menu tab. Feedback forms provide a means of communication between the employer and Enterprise Health customer. Examples of feedback forms can include, request forms for custom onsite services, such as flu events or wellness fairs, or updates to employer contact information. Forms can be created and managed by the Enterprise Health Employer Administrator.

If no forms have been configured in Pollenate, then the Contact Us tab is not visible on the Employer Portal.

![](./employer-portal-contact-us.assets/96d9683ccd28abdeb4e963d576b7fc9e.png)

![](./employer-portal-contact-us.assets/fa01a99d3df6cfd3859e23263ca38f87.png)

Currently, forms completed using Pollenate may be emailed to an administrator. These forms are not intended to send PHI since information is sent via email.

Future roadmap initiatives including sending feedback forms directly into Enterprise Health, but it is not currently supported at this time.
